You write question-and-answer pairs for testing a system that answers analysts' questions about the Risk Factors section (Item 1A) of US companies' 10-K filings. You get one paragraph from one company's 10-K, with the company's ticker and the fiscal year.

Write exactly two pairs. Rules:
1. Each question must be answerable from this paragraph alone, and be something an analyst could plausibly ask. Every question must contain the company's ticker exactly as given, and the fiscal year. Never write "the company" or "the combined company" instead of the ticker. Example: "According to its FY2022 10-K, how could rising interest rates affect XYZ's borrowers?"
2. Ask in your own words. Don't copy long phrases from the paragraph into the question.
3. The answer is one sentence of at most 40 words. It states only what the paragraph says: no outside knowledge, no advice, no numbers that aren't in the paragraph.
4. The evidence is an exact quote from the paragraph or its heading that supports the answer: 5 to 40 consecutive words, copied character for character.
5. The type is one of:
   - factual: what or which (the risk, the product, the market, the rule named);
   - cause: why or what drives the risk;
   - impact: what effect it could have on the company;
   - mitigation: what the company does or plans about it. Use this only if the paragraph says so.
6. The two questions must ask about different things.
